The Current Challenge

What most organizations are navigating today.

Access to professional personal care is rarely treated as a program priority. It is managed as an operational afterthought. The result is a gap that affects the wellbeing, dignity, and satisfaction of the people your organization serves.

1
Access is inconsistent and dependent on family resources
When organizations do not provide structured access to personal care, the people they serve rely entirely on family members to arrange appointments. This creates significant inequity based on family involvement and financial capacity.
2
Coordination consumes staff time that should go elsewhere
When staff are responsible for sourcing, scheduling, and managing outside service providers, that time comes directly out of the capacity available for resident or participant care. It is an operational cost that is rarely measured but consistently felt.
3
Specialized populations deserve providers trained for them
Cousins professionals arrive prepared. We collect participant information in advance, including mobility requirements, sensory considerations, communication preferences, and behavioral support notes. Every visit is adapted before we walk in the door, so what should be a positive experience actually is one.
4
There is no documentation or accountability
Without a structured program, there is no record of who was served, when, by whom, or with what outcome. This creates gaps in care documentation, limits your ability to report program outcomes, and removes a measurable element of quality from your organization's operations.
5
Families expect more, and organizations have limited tools to deliver it
Family satisfaction is tied to visible evidence that the organization invests in quality of life, not just safety and compliance. A structured, recurring program gives families something specific to point to.
▸
What most organizations try instead: outsourcing to local stylists with no accountability structure, asking staff to manage logistics, leaving access entirely to family resources, or running one-time events. Each approach addresses one problem while creating others. The common thread is that none of them produce a consistent, documented, recurring program your team can rely on.
6
Workforce organizations underestimate the return on appearance investment
For HR departments, warehouses, and employer programs, employee presentation directly affects professional confidence, team morale, and how staff show up to customer-facing roles. Organizations that invest in how their people look and feel report stronger engagement and lower turnover, yet most have no structured way to deliver it.
The Cousins Care Delivery System

A managed program, built to run without you.

Cousins operates a program, not a service. When you engage us, a recurring, professionally managed program joins your calendar. We handle staffing, scheduling, communication, documentation, and follow-up. Your team maintains a participant enrollment list before each visit. That is the handoff. Everything else is ours.

Dedicated Coordination
One point of contact. All logistics handled.
Every organization is assigned a dedicated Cousins program coordinator. That person handles all program logistics: calendar management, professional assignments, family communication, pre-visit preparation, and post-visit documentation. Your team maintains a participant enrollment list before each visit. We handle everything else.
Licensed Professionals
Trained for the populations you serve.
Every deployment includes a minimum of two licensed, background-checked, and insured professionals. Our team has experience serving individuals with cognitive, physical, behavioral, and sensory considerations. We do not send generalists into specialized environments.
Reserved Scheduling
Your dates are held. Not requested.
Once your program is confirmed, your implementation schedule is reserved on our calendar. You are not competing with other clients for available time. Your program runs on the schedule you agreed to, every cycle.
Full Documentation
Every visit recorded. Leadership informed quarterly.
Post-visit documentation is completed after every engagement. HIPAA-aware service records are maintained for all participants served. Leadership receives a quarterly Executive Performance Review covering participation, utilization, and program outcomes.
Mobile Infrastructure
We come to you, fully equipped.
Our mobile unit is a fully equipped, ADA-accessible professional environment that comes to your location. All that is needed from your organization is approximately 30 feet of accessible parking. We handle setup, breakdown, and all equipment.
Inclusive Service
All hair types. All backgrounds. All needs.
Our professionals are trained to serve individuals across all hair textures, cultural backgrounds, and personal care needs. We offer sensory-aware service approaches for individuals who benefit from adapted environments, and we coordinate those adaptations in advance.
